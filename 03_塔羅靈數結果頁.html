01 · Birth Cards

你的六張本命牌Your six core cards

這六張牌共同構成你的生命輪廓。它們依照原始順序展開，不替每一個位置預設固定的人生角色。Together, these six cards form the outline of your life. They're shown in their original order, with no fixed role assigned to any position.
每張牌都代表你的一種生命模式，相互映照之下，才慢慢看見你與人生互動的方式。Each card represents one of your life patterns — seeing them reflect off each other is what slowly reveals how you engage with your life.
02 · Hidden Cards

你的隱藏牌Your hidden cards

那些不一定第一眼被看見，卻深深影響你的內在模式。The inner patterns that aren't always visible at first glance, but shape you deeply all the same.
這些模式不一定會一直出現在日常裡，卻可能在特定的環境、關係與生命階段中，被慢慢勾出來。These patterns don't necessarily show up in daily life all the time — but certain environments, relationships and life stages can slowly draw them out.
有些模式不一定容易被察覺，卻常常在你最重要的選擇裡悄悄出現。Some patterns aren't easy to notice, but they quietly show up in the choices that matter most to you.
03 · Your Timing

今年，時間把你帶到這裡This year, time brought you here

流年不是一張牌的單獨預測，而是當年度模式與你的本命結構彼此相遇後，形成的階段性提醒。時間導航，不是再一次的人格解讀。A yearly cycle isn't a single card's standalone prediction — it's the stage-based signal that appears when this year's pattern meets your core structure. Think of it as timing guidance, not another personality reading.
